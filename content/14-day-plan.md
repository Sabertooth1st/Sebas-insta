# 14-day plan

The goal is one sale, not a follower count. Followers are how you get there, not the
target. Everything below assumes you post daily and that the link in your bio points
at the landing page in this repo.

## The shape of it

- **Days 1-4** — post daily, find out which format people actually watch.
- **Days 5-9** — double down on the format that worked, start naming the problem you solve.
- **Days 10-14** — the product goes live and you sell it to people who already trust you.

## Days 1-4: find the format

Post once a day. Four different formats, so you learn something instead of guessing:

1. A result or a before/after. Something finished, shown fast.
2. How you did one small piece of it. Under 30 seconds.
3. An opinion most people in your space would push back on.
4. The story of the worst mistake you made doing this.

Check which one held attention longest. That is your format. Watch time matters more
than likes here, because it is what decides whether the post keeps getting shown.

## Days 5-9: repeat what worked

Keep making the winning format. Change the subject each time, not the structure.

Somewhere in these five days, say out loud what problem you help with. Not a pitch,
just naming it. People cannot buy a solution to a problem they have not heard you
describe.

End two of these posts by asking what people are stuck on. The answers tell you what
to sell, in their words, which is also how you should name it.

## Days 10-14: sell

By now you know what people keep asking about. Make the smallest possible thing that
answers it:

- A guide or checklist, if the problem is that they do not know the steps.
- A template or preset pack, if the problem is that doing it takes too long.

Small is the point. A focused thing that solves one problem sells better than a big
one that solves five, and you can build it in a day.

Price it at $9 to $19 for a first product. Low enough that nobody has to think about
it, high enough to be worth the work.

Then: put it on Gumroad, point the landing page at it, and tell people it exists.
Post about it more than once. Most people who follow you will not see any given post.

## Hooks that work

The first line decides whether anything else gets read. These are the patterns worth
reusing:

- "I spent [time] doing X so you don't have to."
- "Nobody talks about [thing], and it is why [problem] keeps happening."
- "If you're [doing the common thing], stop."
- "[Result] in [timeframe]. Here's exactly how."
- "The mistake that cost me [specific thing]."

Be specific. A real number beats a vague claim every time.

## What to write down

Keep a row per post: date, format, hook, views, watch time, saves, follows gained.
Five minutes a week, and after two weeks you stop guessing about what to post.

Saves and shares matter more than likes. They are what tells the algorithm the post
was worth someone's time.

## What this plan does not do

It will not build an audience from zero in two weeks. If the account is new, the
realistic outcome here is a working format, a clear idea of what to sell, and the
product ready to go. The sales follow the audience, and the audience takes longer
than fourteen days.

If the account already has engaged followers, the day 10-14 stretch is where the
money actually happens, and it can happen fast.
