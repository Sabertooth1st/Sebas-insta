# Products

The paid products are deliberately **not** in this repository.

This repo is public. Anything committed here can be downloaded by anyone, so
putting a product people are meant to pay for in it gives it away. The guide
PDFs, the preset files and the customer zips are kept in the project's own
files instead, and are delivered to buyers through Gumroad.

## What is planned

**From 177** — a guide to the owner's own weight transformation, from 177 kg.
**Not written yet**; the target is about twelve pages plus a printable one-page
summary, but no page count is fixed and none should be advertised until the
thing exists. $19 for the first week of selling, $29 after — the week runs from
launch day, and `content/14-day-plan.md` puts the rise on day 17. This is the
main product.

**Sebastian Signature** — ten Lightroom presets with an install guide. $12 for
the first week, $19 after. Built before the page had an angle; still sellable,
no longer the main play. Its card is parked in a comment on the landing page
until the guide has launched, so that the mailing list promises one thing
rather than two.

## What product material is in this repo

Of the product material: the images, and only the images. `before.jpg` and `after.jpg` are the plain
pair the landing page lays out itself. `assets/before-after.jpg`,
`assets/before-after-story.jpg` and `assets/progress-three-stage.jpg` are the
captioned versions for posting to Instagram. Those are all meant to be public:
they are the page's main piece of content and the product's proof.

The photos in them are the owner's own, grouped by his labels: the 177 kg
panel comes from the set he labelled AT 177KG and the current panel from
CURRENTLY. Head size is matched across panels so the comparison is honest
rather than flattering. If these are ever rebuilt, keep to those labels - an
earlier version put "177 KG" under a photo he had labelled as progress, which
was wrong.

Nothing else product-related belongs here. The guide source, the preset files
and the customer zips live outside git — if you need them and cannot find them,
ask the owner rather than reconstructing them here.

The prices above are also printed on the cards in `index.html`. If one changes,
change both.
